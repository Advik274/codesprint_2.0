## PHASE 1 — THE GAUNTLET
### 🧠 Individual Speed + Logic | ~50 minutes

This is the filter. Everyone starts. Most will fall.

### How It Works
1. A question appears on your screen simultaneously for all teams
2. You have **20–45 seconds** to submit your answer
3. The faster you answer correctly, the more points you earn
4. At the end of each round, the admin eliminates the lowest-scoring teams
5. 5 rounds total — survive all 5 to advance

### Scoring
```
Your Score = Base Points + (Time Limit − Time You Took)
```
Example: 20-point question, 40s limit, answered in 10s → 20 + 30 = **50 points**

### Question Types
| Round | Type | Example |
|-------|------|---------|
| 1–2 | Multiple Choice | "Which data structure uses LIFO?" |
| 3–4 | Multiple Choice | "What is the time complexity of binary search?" |
| 5 | Text Input | "What does [1,2,3].reduce((a,b)=>a+b, 0) return?" |

### Rules
- No googling. No asking friends outside your team.
- First submission counts — no re-attempts per round.
- If you don't submit before timer → 0 points for that round.
- Admin decides how many teams to cut per round.

## PHASE 2 — CAMPUS CONQUEST
### 🗺️ Territory Battle | 90 minutes

Your campus is the battlefield. Your battery is your life.

### Setup
- The campus has **6 Strategic Zones** (each with one of our team members stationed there)
- Your team starts with **100% Battery Life**
- Battery drains **2% per minute** — constantly

### The Goal
Capture the most zones before 90 minutes is up. Top 4–5 teams advance to Phase 3.

### How to Capture a Zone
1. **Physically walk** to a campus zone as a team
2. Enter your **team passcode** on the laptop at that zone
3. **Important:** This costs you **5% battery** (non-refundable)
4. A coding/logic challenge appears
5. **You and your team solve it together** — then type your answer directly on the laptop
6. **Correct?** → Zone captured! Your battery goes up **+20%**
7. **Wrong?** → Zone stays neutral. Your 5% is gone. Try again or go elsewhere.

### Defending Your Zone
Once you capture a zone, **you must set a trap** for other teams:
- Go to your team dashboard
- Select "Set Defense Problem" for that zone
- Pick 1 challenge from 5 options — this becomes what attackers must solve to steal from you
- Choose wisely! Make it hard but fair.

### Stealing an Enemy Zone
1. Go to an enemy zone (one that another team owns)
2. Enter your passcode (costs 5% battery)
3. The defending team's challenge appears
4. Solve it → zone is yours, +20% battery
5. Wrong → zone stays with the defender

### Battery Rules
```
Start: 100%
Every minute: -2%
Attempt a zone: -5%
Capture a zone: +20%
Hit 0%: ELIMINATED
```

Warnings are sent at 20% and 10% — act fast.

### Important Zone Rules
- **Only one team at a time** can attempt a zone — if someone is already there, wait
- The zone laptop will show "ZONE BUSY" — you'll see it
- Zone operators are **neutral** — they cannot help you solve the challenge
- If you have a dispute, raise your hand and call a coordinator

## PHASE 3 — FINAL BUILD
### 💻 Build Under Pressure | 2.5 hours

Only the top teams make it here. Now prove you can build.

### What Happens
1. The admin reveals a **problem statement** — your mission
2. Your team has **2.5 hours** to build a working web application
3. Submit your project via the team dashboard before time runs out

### What to Submit
- **Deployment URL** — a live link (Vercel, Netlify, Railway, etc.)
- **GitHub URL** — your code repository
- **Notes** — features built, tech stack, anything judges should know

You can resubmit as many times as you want. **Last submission before timer ends counts.**

### How You'll Be Judged
| Criteria | Weight | What Judges Look For |
|----------|--------|---------------------|
| UI Design | 30% | Clean, intuitive, responsive |
| Functionality | 40% | Features work, no crashes, handles edge cases |
| Problem Fit | 30% | Did you actually solve the given problem? |

**Maximum score: 100 points**

### Rules
- Any tech stack is allowed (React, Django, Flask, Node, PHP, whatever)
- Internet access is allowed for documentation
- No using pre-made full project templates — original work only
- All team members must contribute
- Judges' decisions are final

## ⚔️ CODE ARENA 2026 MISSIONS
### (These rounds run when the event is CODE ARENA — your coordinator will tell you which rounds are on)

**Format:** 3 missions, 100 marks total. One laptop per team for Bug Hunt and Code Strike. Team discussion allowed. AI tools NOT allowed. No plagiarism.

### MISSION 1 — INTELLIGENCE SCAN · 25 MCQs · 15 min · 20 marks
- Topics: Java, C/C++, Python, DBMS, OS, Git, HTML/CSS, Aptitude, Logical Reasoning
- Every member answers on their own device; your team score is the **average** across members who submit — everyone participating beats one person carrying
- **Pick your name from your team's list** — no typing, so nobody's answers get filed under a misspelling. If your name isn't there, tell a coordinator and it appears within seconds
- Submit before the timer — unanswered = 0 for those questions

### MISSION 2 — BUG HUNT · 5 buggy programs · 20 min · 30 marks
- Five short programs in Java / C++ / Python — each has a planted syntax or logic bug
- Read the code, find the bug, and **type the corrected line** in the box under each program
- **One submission per team** — discuss first, lock in once. 6 marks per correct fix
- Spacing and capitalisation don't matter; the fix itself does

### MISSION 3 — CODE STRIKE · 4 problems · 40 min · 50 marks
- Easy (8) / Medium (12) / Medium (12) / Extreme (18) revealed at once — solve in any language, any editor
- ⌨️ **Type your solution into the platform — pasting is disabled**, and leaving the tab is recorded as a violation just like the earlier rounds. Plan on paper, type it in.
- Re-submitting is allowed until time runs out, but the latest version resets your submission time
- Scoring per problem: **70% correctness + 20% efficiency (judged) + 10% time bonus** (earlier = more)
- Test against the sample inputs before pasting — judges run the samples first

### 🔒 PROTECTED MODE
When the organisers enable Protected Mode, your event tab locks down during the timed rounds (Intelligence Scan, Bug Hunt, tie-breaks):
- **Copy, paste and right-click are disabled** on the event page
- **Switching tabs or apps is detected and reported to the admin the moment it happens** — you'll see "🚨 Violation recorded" and so will they
- Violations escalate: **1st = warning** (no marks lost) → **2nd = yellow card** (no marks lost) → **3rd and beyond = −2 marks each**, capped at −10
- The first two are free because phones ring and notifications happen — but after that it costs you
- If two teams finish level, the team with **fewer violations places higher**
- Your card is shown only to you and the organisers, never on the big screen
- Code Strike is the exception — you're expected to switch to your editor there

### TIE BREAKER ⚡
- If teams are tied, a rapid-fire question appears **only on the tied teams' screens**
- One attempt per team — **fastest correct answer wins** the higher place

## GENERAL RULES

1. **No cheating.** Sharing answers between teams = immediate disqualification.
2. **Respect the zones.** Don't crowd the zone area with more members than necessary.
3. **Keep it fair.** When setting defense problems, write clear, solvable challenges — no trick questions designed to be impossible.
4. **One team = one attempt per zone at a time.** Don't send half your team while the other half attempts elsewhere.
5. **Device issues are your responsibility.** Charge your phone. Use your own data if hotspot is slow.
6. **Admin is final.** No arguments with the admin's decisions.
7. **Have fun.** This is a competition, but it's also a party. Enjoy it.
